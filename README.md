# PharmaGPT

AI Scientist for Drug Discovery and Biomedical Research

---

## Vision

PharmaGPT aims to build an AI system capable of understanding molecules, proteins, biomedical literature, and drug interactions.

The long-term goal is to create a foundation model for drug discovery that assists researchers in identifying promising compounds, predicting molecular interactions, evaluating toxicity, discovering new therapeutic opportunities, and accelerating biomedical research.

---

## Why PharmaGPT?

Drug discovery is one of the most challenging scientific problems in the world.

Developing a new drug can take years of research and billions of dollars. Modern AI techniques such as Graph Neural Networks (GNNs), Transformers, and Foundation Models have the potential to significantly accelerate this process.

PharmaGPT aims to combine molecular intelligence, protein understanding, and biomedical knowledge into a unified AI system for drug discovery.
